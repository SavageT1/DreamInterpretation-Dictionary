How to use it

Put the video on before you get into bed, with the volume low. Let the narration guide your breathing, and let the music carry you toward sleep. If you wake up during the night, you can pick it up again.

Sleep and lucid dreaming

A calm mind makes dream recall easier. If you are practicing lucid dreaming, pair a relaxed bedtime with a simple intention and a dream journal next to your bed. The lucid dreaming guide explains reality checks and other common techniques.

Explore the symbols

Once you have a dream, look up its symbols in the dream dictionary, or read about common themes like flying, falling, and being chased.

Sleep questions

Is this a medical treatment? No. It is a relaxation recording, not medical advice. If you have trouble sleeping, nightmares, or a mental health concern, talk to a doctor.
