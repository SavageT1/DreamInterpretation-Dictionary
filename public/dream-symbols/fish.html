What It Means to Dream About Fish

Fish in dreams often stand for emotion, the unconscious and the abundance that moves beneath the surface.

Interpret your own dream →

Fish live in water, which often stands for feeling. A fish in a dream can be a feeling you are catching, avoiding or simply noticing.

Common interpretations

Emotion beneath the surface. Fish swim unseen, a reminder that feelings can be present even when you are not aware of them.

Abundance. A catch or a school of fish can reflect opportunity or plenty.

Catching or avoiding. Catching a fish may mean grasping a feeling; letting it go may mean releasing it.

Intuition. Fish in clear water may suggest insight arriving from your unconscious.

The emotional tone matters

Calm watching fish swim. A relaxed relationship with your feelings.

Distaste eating fish. Discomfort with something you have taken in or accepted.

Excitement catching a big fish. An opportunity or feeling you are glad to have grasped.

Common variations

A dead fish. A feeling or opportunity that has faded.

A fish out of water. Feeling out of your element.

Fishing alone. Patient pursuit of something important to you.

Questions to ask yourself

  1. What feeling is swimming just beneath the surface?
  2. Am I trying to catch something or let it go?
  3. Where is abundance showing up?
  4. What does this water feel like?
  5. What is my intuition noticing?
