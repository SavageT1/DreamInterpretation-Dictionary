Dream symbols guide

Dream symbols are starting points, not fixed answers.

A symbol can mean something different from one person to the next. The most useful interpretation considers the whole dream, the emotion you felt, and what the image means in your own life.

Common symbols to reflect on

Water

Water may connect with emotion, change, calm, uncertainty, or a recent memory. Ask how the water felt and what you were doing in it.

Flying

Flying dreams may invite reflection on freedom, confidence, escape, or the feeling of being out of control.

Houses and rooms

A house can represent familiarity, identity, privacy, or different parts of your life. Notice which room stood out.

Being chased

Being chased may reflect pressure, avoidance, fear, or a problem asking for attention. The emotion matters more than a universal definition.
